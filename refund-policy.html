Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This policy explains when you can get a refund for an AIMAZZE subscription, and what happens when you cancel.

1. First-purchase refund window

If you're purchasing a paid AIMAZZE plan (Starter, Pro, or Elite) for the first time, you can request a full refund within 7 days of your purchase date, no questions asked. This applies whether you've used the local AI models, connected your own API keys, or simply tried the app and decided it isn't for you.

To request a refund, email hello@aimazze.com with the email address used for purchase. Refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

This 7-day window applies once per person, to your first paid subscription only.

2. Renewals and recurring charges

Subscription renewals (monthly or annual) are not eligible for the 7-day refund window described above. If you no longer want to continue your subscription, cancel before your next billing date — see Section 4 below.

If a renewal charge occurs because you forgot to cancel, contact us at hello@aimazze.com within 48 hours of the charge and we'll review it on a case-by-case basis.

3. Annual plans

Annual subscriptions follow the same 7-day first-purchase window as monthly plans. After that window, annual plans are non-refundable for the remainder of the billing year, but you can still cancel auto-renewal at any time — see Section 4.

4. How to cancel

To cancel, email hello@aimazze.com with the email address used for purchase. Cancelling stops future renewals — it does not retroactively refund the current billing period unless you're within the 7-day first-purchase window.

When your current paid period ends, your account automatically moves to the Free tier. Nothing is deleted: your chat history, Vault documents, and settings all remain on your device exactly as they were.

5. Lifetime purchases

Lifetime plan purchases (where available) follow the same 7-day first-purchase refund window. After 7 days, Lifetime purchases are non-refundable, since they represent a one-time payment with no recurring charge to cancel.

6. What's not covered

  • Charges resulting from your own decision to upgrade, downgrade, or switch billing cycles after the 7-day window
  • Dissatisfaction with the output quality of a third-party AI model accessed through your own API key (BYOK) — this is between you and that provider
  • Inability to use AIMAZZE due to your own hardware, internet, or operating system limitations, where this was reasonably disclosed in our system requirements
AIMAZZE is a desktop application. There is no physical product to ship or return — see our Shipping & Delivery Policy for how access is provided after purchase.

7. Questions

If anything here is unclear, or your situation doesn't fit neatly into the categories above, just email us at hello@aimazze.com — we read every message and will work with you directly.
