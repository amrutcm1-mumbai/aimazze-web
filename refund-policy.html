Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This policy explains how payment, renewal, and cancellation work at AIMAZZE — including the fact that we don't offer refunds for a change of mind.

1. Your first payment

When you buy an AIMAZZE plan — Starter, Pro, or Elite — that payment is final, including your first one. We don't offer refunds for a change of mind, and we'd rather say so plainly here than leave you to find out later.

What you do have is full control over what comes next. Cancel any time before your renewal date and you won't be charged again. Everything you've already paid for stays yours until that period ends — then your paid features switch off, and you keep whatever the Free tier offers.

If you're not ready to commit, don't. The Free tier isn't a countdown — it's a real plan you can stay on as long as you like, with no card on file. Try AIMAZZE properly there, and upgrade when you're sure.

If a charge is wrong, that's different. A duplicate charge, or an amount that doesn't match the plan you chose, is our mistake and we'll correct it. Email support@aimazze.com with the address you bought with and we'll sort it out.

2. Renewals and recurring charges

Your plan renews on its own so the app keeps working without you having to think about it. Renewal charges are final in the same way your first payment is.

Because a charge can't be undone, we make sure none of them arrives unannounced. Three days before every renewal — monthly or annual — we email you a reminder: what's renewing, how much, and the exact date. If you'd rather stop there, just reply to that email and tell us — one message is enough to make sure the next charge never happens.

Cancel before the renewal date and that's the end of the charges. Cancel after it and the period you've just paid for is yours in full — you keep every feature until it ends, and nothing is charged after that. You're never paying for time you can't use.

Billing questions: support@aimazze.com. If a renewal charge itself looks wrong — the wrong amount, or charged twice — see the note at the end of Section 1; that's ours to fix.

3. Annual plans

Annual plans work the same way, with the same reminder three days before each renewal. The payment for the year is final once it's made, and cancelling stops next year's charge rather than refunding this one.

In practice that means cancelling in month three doesn't cut you off in month three. You keep everything through month twelve — then your paid features switch off, and you keep whatever the Free tier offers. The year you paid for is the year you get.

We spell this out because twelve months is a real commitment. If you'd rather not make it yet, a monthly plan is the lower-commitment way in, and the Free tier costs nothing at all.

Billing questions: support@aimazze.com

4. How to cancel

Email support@aimazze.com from the address you bought with and tell us you'd like to stop your subscription. We'll confirm by reply. That's the whole process — one email, and we take it from there.

Send it at least 24 hours before your renewal date. Cancelling works at any time, including the last hour before a renewal — we don't close the exit when you most want it. But once a payment is in flight with your bank it can't always be pulled back, and a day's margin avoids that situation entirely.

Cancelling doesn't cut anything off straight away, and that's deliberate. You keep every feature through the end of the period you've already paid for — then your paid features switch off, and you keep whatever the Free tier offers. Nothing is deleted either: your chat history, Vault documents, and settings live on your device, not our servers, so they stay exactly where they are whatever happens to your plan.

Billing questions: support@aimazze.com

5. Lifetime

Lifetime is a single payment with no renewal, no billing cycle, and nothing to cancel. It's closed to new purchases, and everyone already holding one keeps it.

"Lifetime" means the lifetime of the product: for as long as AIMAZZE is offered, your access doesn't expire and you are never billed again. We'd rather define the word than let it float — it isn't a promise about a specific number of years, because nobody can honestly make that promise about software.

What we can commit to is not winding it down quietly. If AIMAZZE were discontinued, or the Lifetime tier retired, we'd contact Lifetime holders directly and give reasonable advance notice — not leave you to discover it when something stops working one morning.

The payment is final, in the same way every other payment on this page is. There's no renewal to stop, and nothing to refund.

Questions about a Lifetime purchase: support@aimazze.com

6. What we don't cover

Because payments are final, this section is mostly about where the line sits — and one side of it matters more than the other.

Anything that's our error, we fix. A duplicate charge, an amount that doesn't match your plan, a plan you didn't pick, a payment that went through but never activated your licence. Email support@aimazze.com and we'll correct it.

What we can't undo:

  • A change of mind, or a plan you stopped using.
  • Output quality from a third-party AI model you've connected with your own API key. That's between you and that provider — the traffic never passes through us.
  • Hardware, operating system, or connectivity limits on your own machine, where our published system requirements set the expectation before you bought.
  • Charges from changing plan or billing cycle yourself. The new plan starts, the old one stops, and neither is reversed.
AIMAZZE is a desktop application. There is no physical product to ship or return — see our Shipping & Delivery Policy for how access is provided after purchase.

7. Questions

If anything here is unclear, or your situation doesn't fit neatly into the categories above, just email us at hello@aimazze.com — we read every message and will work with you directly.
