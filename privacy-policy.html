Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information AmrynNova Private Limited ("AmrynNova", "we", "us") collects when you use AIMAZZE, and how it's handled. AIMAZZE is built local-first — most of what you'd expect a typical app to collect, we simply don't, because it never reaches our servers in the first place.

1. What stays on your device — and never reaches us

The following data is stored exclusively on your local device and is never transmitted to AmrynNova's servers, regardless of which AI model or provider you use:

Data typeWhere it lives
Chat history and messagesLocal SQLite database on your device
Vault documents you uploadLocal storage on your device
Your API keys (if using BYOK)OS-level secure storage on your device
Avatar, zone, and theme preferencesLocal app storage on your device

When you use AIMAZZE's built-in local AI models, your prompts and the model's responses never leave your machine. When you use your own API key (BYOK) to connect to a provider like Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google, your conversation routes directly from your device to that provider — AmrynNova is never in that data path and does not receive, store, or process that content. That said, this is not the same as blanket privacy: once your data reaches the provider, their own data-handling terms govern it, not ours. See How AIMAZZE Protects Your Data for the full breakdown by tier.

2. What we do collect

To operate the business side of AIMAZZE — license activation, billing, and support — we collect a limited amount of information:

  • Email address and name — collected at purchase, used to issue and validate your license key, and to send activation/billing emails
  • Subscription tier and billing cycle — so we know what plan you're on
  • License key and activation status — managed via our licensing provider, Keygen.sh
  • Payment information — processed directly by our payment processor (Razorpay); AmrynNova does not store your card or bank details

That's the complete list. We do not collect your chat content, documents, or AI provider API keys.

3. Free-tier usage data

To enforce daily usage limits on the Free tier, we track a message count per day against a locally-generated device identifier via our backend (Cloudflare Workers + KV). This is a usage counter only — it does not include the content of your messages, only that a message was sent.

4. Third-party services we use

ServicePurpose
Keygen.shLicense key generation and validation
RazorpayPayment processing
ResendTransactional emails (activation, receipts)
CloudflareWebsite hosting and backend infrastructure

If you choose to connect your own API key to an AI provider (BYOK), your use of that provider is governed by their own privacy policy and terms — not ours. We encourage you to review your chosen provider's data handling commitments, particularly for sensitive or privileged work.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of the limited data we hold about you (email, name, subscription status), or request its deletion, by emailing hello@aimazze.com. Since your chat history and documents live entirely on your own device, you control and can delete that data at any time by uninstalling AIMAZZE or clearing the app's local storage — we have no copy to delete on our end.

6. Data retention

We retain your email, name, and license/billing records for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as required for tax, accounting, and legal compliance purposes.

7. Children's privacy

AIMAZZE is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Email us at hello@aimazze.com.

AIMAZZE is built on the principle that the least amount of data we can responsibly collect is the right amount. If something here ever seems to drift from that, tell us.
