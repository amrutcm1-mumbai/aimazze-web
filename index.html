Data & privacy
Three ways to work with AI.
Only one of them is truly private.

Most AI tools ask you to trust their servers. AIMAZZE is built so you don't have to guess.

🔒
Local Models — Never leaves your device
Runs entirely on your machine. No prompt, document, or output is transmitted anywhere. This is the only tier that's private by architecture, not by policy.
🔑
Bring Your Own Key (Cloud) — AIMAZZE never in the middle
Your prompts go straight from your device to the AI provider you choose, using your own API key. AIMAZZE never receives, stores, or transmits your data. But you're still bound by that provider's own terms — read them before sending anything privileged or regulated.
⚠️
Public Consumer AI Tools — What most professionals are already exposed to
Free consumer chatbots and browser extensions often use your inputs for training, retain them indefinitely, or share them with third parties. This is the category regulators and courts have already started scrutinizing when privileged or client data ends up there.

Quick definition first: an API key is a personal access credential that a provider (like Anthropic or OpenAI) gives you so their service knows it's your account making the request. BYOK means Bring Your Own Key — paste that key into AIMAZZE Settings, and your conversations route directly from your device to that provider's API. AIMAZZE is never in that data path — but once your data reaches the provider, their terms govern it, not ours, so it's worth a quick read of their API data policy for anything sensitive.
