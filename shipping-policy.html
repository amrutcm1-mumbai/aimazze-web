Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

AIMAZZE is a fully digital desktop application. There is no physical product, and nothing is ever shipped to you. This page explains how you actually get access to AIMAZZE after purchase.

1. Delivery method

After completing payment, delivery happens in two parts:

  • Download: the AIMAZZE desktop application is downloaded directly from our website or from GitHub Releases, available immediately — no waiting period.
  • License activation: within minutes of a successful purchase, you'll receive an activation email at the address you provided at checkout, containing a link that opens AIMAZZE and activates your license automatically. If you already have AIMAZZE installed, you can also activate manually using your license key under Settings → Plan & Billing.

2. Delivery timeframe

Access to your paid plan is typically available immediately after payment confirmation. In rare cases involving payment verification delays, activation may take up to a few hours. If you haven't received your activation email within 24 hours of a successful payment, contact hello@aimazze.com and we'll resolve it promptly.

3. Supported platforms

AIMAZZE is currently available for Windows, with macOS support coming soon. Make sure your system meets the minimum requirements listed on our download page before purchasing.

4. No physical shipping

Because AIMAZZE involves no physical goods, there are no shipping carriers, shipping fees, customs charges, or delivery addresses involved at any point in the purchase process.

5. Activation issues

If your activation email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder first. If it's still missing, or the activation link doesn't work as expected, email hello@aimazze.com with your purchase email and we'll get you activated manually.

For questions about refunds related to delivery or activation problems, see our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

6. Contact

Any questions about getting access to AIMAZZE? Email hello@aimazze.com.
